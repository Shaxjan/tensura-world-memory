# Dwargon students — individual character registry

There are 22 students in the current theater group.

## Core rule

They are 22 separate people, not one collective NPC. A student may have a different temperament, skill, opinion, friendship, rivalry, humor style, fear, ambition and relationship with Arlequino than the others.

At present, durable memory has recovered the names **Фира**, **Тиа** and other specific identities may be re-established later. Unrecovered names must not be invented. The fact that a name is not remembered by Arlequino is not proof that the person has no name.

## Individual slots

Until a student's identity is causally re-established, use stable internal slots only:

`student_01` through `student_22`.

Each slot accumulates evidence independently.

## Per-student record to accumulate

- name, once causally re-established;
- visible role / skills;
- stable temperament;
- recurring manner of speaking;
- preferences and dislikes;
- relationships with other students;
- relationship with Arlequino;
- memories of shared scenes;
- independent goals and plans;
- demonstrated strengths and weaknesses;
- boundaries / things the student refuses;
- current knowledge;
- current unknowns.

## Depth

A student starts at D0/D1. Depth increases after repeated meaningful scenes. A student who appears often may eventually reach D3/D4 and support nuanced autonomous behavior. A background student remains shallow until the story gives evidence.

## Group realism

Silence is a valid group reaction. So is disagreement, cross-talk, awkwardness, fatigue, curiosity, boredom, private conversation, or one student's initiative. Do not give all 22 students simultaneous jokes or synchronized reactions.

When only a subset is present, narrate only the observed subset. Do not claim what absent students are doing unless a causal channel reveals it.
