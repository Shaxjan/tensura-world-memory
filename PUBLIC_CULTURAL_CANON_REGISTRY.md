# Canon safety rules

1. Do not convert capacity into attendance.
2. Do not convert promotional audience claims into observed crowds.
3. Do not convert archived song text into proof that a retconned performance completed.
4. Do not merge similarly timed concerts without direct identity evidence.
5. Do not infer a complete setlist from general repertoire.
6. Preserve `UNKNOWN` when the surviving source does not support precision.
7. Newer direct player corrections and active runtime facts outrank this historical registry.
8. For detailed provenance, use `recovery/RECOVERY_INDEX.md`.
