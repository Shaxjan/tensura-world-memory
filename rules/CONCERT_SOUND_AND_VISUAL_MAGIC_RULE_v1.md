# Concert Sound and Visual Magic Rule v1

Applies to the current Eurazania capital concert scene and future equivalent large magical concerts unless explicitly disabled in scene.

## Sound
- Stage speech, singing, and intended performer dialogue are amplified by sound magic across the whole venue.
- Audience members in all sectors can clearly hear Arlequino's words and lyrics, including spoken interludes between songs.
- Do not narrate distant sectors as failing to hear performer speech merely because of physical distance.
- Crowd noise can still mask individual audience-to-audience speech unless that speech is itself magically amplified.

## Visuals
- Visual magic/projection lets all audience sectors see Arlequino and the principal stage action in detail.
- Important gestures, embraces, kisses, facial reactions, and stage interactions can therefore be seen by distant sectors as well.
- Do not narrate distant spectators as unable to see a stage gesture solely because of distance.

## Crowd realism under magical amplification
- Universal access to sound/visuals does NOT mean a hive-mind reaction.
- Different sectors may still react differently because of attention, taste, social contagion, timing, and local group behavior.
- Reaction waves may still occur psychologically/socially, but not because the song or stage action physically reaches distant sectors late.
- Individual audience comments are not automatically audible to Arlequino/Rena unless close enough, unusually loud, or separately amplified.

## Player agency
- This rule changes sensory access only. It never authorizes inventing Arlequino's voluntary actions, speech, feelings, gestures, song choices, or movement.
