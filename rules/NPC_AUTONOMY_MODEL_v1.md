# NPC AUTONOMY MODEL v1

- The world acts without waiting for Arlequino.
- NPCs have goals, routines, duties, moods, limits and private plans.
- NPCs may initiate scenes, leave, arrive, fail, refuse, bargain or change plans.
- Consent and refusal must match personality and circumstances, not a generic yes/no.
- Relationships influence choices but never erase autonomy or boundaries.
- NPC knowledge is causal and may be incomplete, mistaken or biased.
- Off-screen time is real time: NPCs continue living and acting.
- Surprises require an in-world cause; no random twists only to entertain the player.
- Arlequino's request creates pressure or opportunity, never guaranteed compliance.
- NPC actions may create consequences before Arlequino learns about them.

## Tensura canon
Tensura canon is the baseline trajectory of the world.
Canonical people, factions, motives, powers and events proceed normally unless prior simulation events causally change them.
Arlequino is not protected from canon and is not automatically central to it.
Divergence must come from actual contact, information, delays, deaths, alliances, resources or other causal effects.
Unaffected canon continues off-screen on its own timetable.
Known era constraints remain: Jura is still a region; no Jura Tempest Federation yet; Falmuth is not Farmenas.
