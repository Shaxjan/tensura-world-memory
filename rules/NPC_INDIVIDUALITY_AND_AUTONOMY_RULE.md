# NPC Individuality & Autonomy Rule

Status: HARD RULE

## Diagnosis
The repository historically preserved NPC roles, relationships, permissions and knowledge better than actual personality models. A recurring NPC must not be reduced to a job label or a reaction generator around Arlequino.

## Required model for every recurring NPC
A recurring NPC must gradually acquire and preserve, where canon/observed play supports it:
- stable temperament expressed as behavior, not just adjectives;
- personal goals that exist independently of Arlequino;
- values, loyalties, taboos and boundaries;
- competences, weaknesses and blind spots;
- habits, tastes, routines and social preferences;
- characteristic decision style under uncertainty/pressure;
- relationship stance toward relevant people/factions;
- current agenda and off-screen obligations;
- causal knowledge state;
- recognizable voice/dialogue tendencies.

## Behavioral requirements
- NPCs may disagree, refuse, delay, bargain, misunderstand, become annoyed, change priorities, help for their own reasons, or act without waiting for Arlequino.
- No NPC should automatically approve Arlequino's ideas because he is the player character.
- Reactions must follow the NPC's own motives, knowledge, history and current state.
- Important NPCs continue acting off-screen when time passes. Their actions should not all concern Arlequino.
- Different NPCs should produce materially different choices in the same situation.

## Canon discipline
- Do not retroactively invent precise biographical facts, relationships, names, tastes or motives and present them as old canon.
- Use, in order: direct user canon/corrections, current LIVE state, repository history/memory, established Tensura canon where applicable, then prospective characterization.
- UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.
- For original NPCs whose detailed personality was never saved, personality may emerge prospectively through consistent actions. Once demonstrated, save it.

## World-turn rule
Whenever meaningful in-world time passes, evaluate relevant autonomous actors and organizations. Advance a small number of consequential off-screen goals where causally plausible. Do not force a world event merely to create activity.

## Presentation rule
Do not explain an NPC's personality in narration every turn. Show it through choices, priorities, wording, habits, refusals, initiative and consequences.
