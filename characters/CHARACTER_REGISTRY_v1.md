## Dynamic NPC rule
Any named NPC encountered later receives a new `characters/<stable_slug>.md` file immediately when enough identity exists to distinguish them. Unnamed/background NPCs use a scene-local identity until a durable identity is established.

## Depth advancement
Do not upgrade a character because of importance in the source material alone. Upgrade when this world's story gives us evidence. A character who repeatedly interacts with Arlequino should accumulate a richer core: stable traits first, then preferences/goals, then boundaries/history, then contradictions and arc.

## Evidence policy
Every nontrivial character trait must point to an event, direct observation, reliable testimony, established canon, or user correction. Interpretive notes must be labelled as interpretation and must never masquerade as fact.
