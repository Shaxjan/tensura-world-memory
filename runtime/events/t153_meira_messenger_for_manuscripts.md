# T+153 — Meira messenger for manuscripts

At approximately T+153 ~12:16 in Meira's workroom, Maestro Arlequino instructed Meira: "отправь к нам домой посыльного, я передам рукописи."

Meaning:
- Arlequino wants Meira to send a messenger to the family home so he can hand over the completed manuscripts for printing workflow.
- This does not itself mean the manuscripts have already been physically transferred; transfer occurs only when the messenger reaches them and Arlequino actually hands the manuscripts over.
- Family home address already established in canon: 18 Old Garden Street / 18 Старая Садовая.
