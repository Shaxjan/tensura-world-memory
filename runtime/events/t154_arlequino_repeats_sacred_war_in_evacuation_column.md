# T+154 — Arlequino repeats «Страна огромная / Священная война» while marching with evacuation column

Time: approximately T+154 ~07:35–07:40.
Location: moving evacuation column leaving Eurazania capital.

Canonical player action:
- Arlequino walks with the evacuation column and performs the same anti-Milim war song again.
- He uses resonance magic to strongly amplify his singing voice over the column.
- This repetition follows immediately after his magically amplified public speech that he believes in the beastmen, their warlike spirit, and Carrion, and that they are not a nation of cowards who will break before a stronger enemy.
- The song functions as wartime morale and mobilization, not as a normal concert.
- Repetition and magical amplification increase the number of direct witnesses and make the refrain easier to carry down the column through ordinary oral transmission.

The full exact lyrics are the same canonical text previously supplied by the player in the T+154 «Страна огромная / Священная война» performance scene and remain unchanged.

Continuity constraints:
- Civilians continue evacuating; morale can rise without the column irrationally abandoning evacuation.
- Reactions vary: fear, pride, defiance, anger, shock, participation, indifference.
- Do not assume Milim personally hears this unless a causal transmission event establishes it.
