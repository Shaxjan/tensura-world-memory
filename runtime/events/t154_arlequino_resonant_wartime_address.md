# T+154 — Arlequino broadcasts resonant wartime address during Eurazania evacuation

Time: approximately T+154 ~07:42–07:43.
Location: mass evacuation columns leaving Eurazania capital.

Context:
- Milim Nava has just publicly appeared and declared war on Carrion.
- The whole capital is evacuating in organized columns.
- Arlequino has already performed the wartime song «Страна огромная» with magical resonance; the song is spreading among hundreds and thousands in the columns.

Player action:
Arlequino deliberately projects his voice loudly with magical resonance so that the speech carries across a very large portion of the evacuation column.

Exact player-supplied address:

«ВНИМАНИЕ ! ГОВОРИТ ЭУРОЗАНИЯ!НАСЕЛЕНИЯ ЭУРОЗАНИИ!СЕГОДНЯ В 7 ЧАСОВ УТРА , БЕЗ КАКОЙ ЛИБО ПРИЧИНЫ , КНЯЖНА ТЬМЫ МИЛИМ НАВА!ВТОРГЛАСЬ В СТОЛИЦУ КОРОЛЕВСТВА ЭУРОЗАНИЯ , И ОБЪЯВИЛА ВОЙНУ !НАЧАЛАСЬ ВЕЛИКАЯ ОТЕЧЕСТВЕННАЯ ВОЙНА ЭУРОЗАНСКОГО НАРОДА ПРОТИВ ДРАКОНЬЕЙ ЗАХВАТЧИЦЫ ! НАШЕ ДЕЛО ПРАВОЕ ! ВРАГ БУДЕТ РАЗБИТ! ПОБЕДА БУДЕТ ЗА НАМИ!»

Interpretation / GM rules:
- Preserve the speech as Arlequino's public claim and rhetoric. Do not silently convert contested/unknown assertions such as «без какой либо причины» into omniscient world fact.
- The address has the character of a wartime proclamation: it names Milim directly, frames the conflict as a national defensive war, invokes homeland and victory, and is delivered immediately after a mass war-song performance.
- Because magical resonance substantially increases reach, the immediate audience is large and the speech can spread rapidly through eyewitness retelling, but knowledge still propagates causally.
- Reactions should reflect Eurazania's martial culture, loyalty to Carrion, the shock of war with Milim, and the fact that civilians are still in evacuation rather than at a rally.
