# T+153 — Maestro Arlequino orders public publicity campaign for Cthulhu books

Time: T+153 ~12:15
Location: Meira's workroom, Eurazania capital

Arlequino explicitly instructs Meira to spread word widely that Maestro plans to begin printing the Cthulhu stories.

Requested publicity channels explicitly named by Arlequino:
- markets;
- combat arenas;
- magic guilds;
- adventurer guilds;
- broadly 'everywhere'.

Important interpretation:
- This is Arlequino's directive/request to Meira, not an instantaneous world-state change.
- Actual spread requires Meira/organizers to accept and execute the campaign through available local channels, messengers, notices, guild contacts, arena contacts, market criers, etc.
- Information propagation should obey NEWS_RUMOR_PROPAGATION_v1.md; people learn gradually through causal channels.
- Public message should be framed as advance notice that Maestro Arlequino plans to begin printing/releasing the Cthulhu stories, not as proof that the 50,000-copy print run is already approved or underway.
- Current proposed publication order remains: 1) «Зов Ктулху», 2) «Маска Ктулху», 3) «Газель и Эмилия», 4) «Лейла и безумец».
- Arlequino proposed 50,000 copies for «Зов Ктулху», but production capacity/cost/order are not yet confirmed.
