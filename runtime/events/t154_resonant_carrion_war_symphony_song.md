Time: approximately T+154 ~07:49 onward
Location: moving southern evacuation columns leaving Eurazania capital

Context:
- Milim has publicly declared war on Carrion.
- Arlequino has already introduced two resonant wartime songs and a mass-address framing the conflict as Eurazania's great patriotic war.
