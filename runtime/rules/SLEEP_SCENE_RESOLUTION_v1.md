# SLEEP_SCENE_RESOLUTION_v1

Effective from T+152 after explicit player correction.

When Arlequino goes to sleep and the player then says he sleeps / continues sleeping, do NOT spend a turn narrating that he is still asleep or that he is gradually waking up.

Resolve sleep as a time-skip to the next meaningful outcome:
- he wakes naturally;
- someone/something wakes him;
- a dangerous event interrupts sleep;
- or, if causally justified, a severe outcome occurs.

The next narrated scene after sleep should begin at the actual wake/interruption/outcome. Hidden events during sleep remain hidden until causally learned.

Do not invent vague pre-wake awareness such as 'consciousness gradually returns' unless the actual wake event itself requires it.

This rule overrides prior sleep narration patterns.
