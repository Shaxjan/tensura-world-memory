# User dialogue punctuation rule — ellipses are in-world pauses

When Arlequino's user-written dialogue contains `...`, those ellipses represent real pauses between his spoken words.

During such a pause:
- It may remain simply a pause, or
- An NPC may naturally speak, react, or act before Arlequino continues with the next user-written phrase.

Do not reorder, delete, or rewrite away the user's intended sequence. Preserve the user's spoken wording and let NPC interjections fit only inside the pause when appropriate.

This is a durable RP interpretation rule explicitly requested by the user.
