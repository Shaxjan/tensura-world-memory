# Player knowledge visibility rule

Canonical simulation rule established on T+138:

- The player only receives information their character can currently perceive or later learn through in-world means.
- While the player character is asleep, unconscious, absent, or otherwise unable to observe events, do not narrate hidden NPC actions or world events to the player as if known.
- Hidden events may still occur in the world, but they remain undisclosed until discovered naturally.
- Previous narration describing Rena's actions during Arlequino's sleep on T+138 is not established as player-known fact and should not be treated as canon unless later revealed through in-world evidence or dialogue.
