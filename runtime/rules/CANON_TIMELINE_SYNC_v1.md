# Canon timeline synchronization v1 — activated at T+151

Status: ACTIVE CANON/RUNTIME RULE.

## Purpose
Synchronize the autonomous Tensura canon layer with Arlequino's T+ day counter so canonical events continue independently of the player and cannot wait for player projects, festivals, travel, romance, sleep, or other local scenes.

## Source/precision rule
Tensura canon provides a reliable event ORDER and several relative intervals, but not a complete absolute calendar for every scene. Therefore this file creates a deterministic simulation mapping in T+ dates without pretending that those T+ numbers are printed calendar dates from the novels.

If later primary/canonical material proves an interval incompatible with this mapping, preserve already-played causality and repair only the smallest future interval required. Never silently move a canonical event merely to protect a player plan.

## Locked synchronization anchor
At current player time T+151, the world is in the late Human–Monster Interaction / immediately pre-crisis phase before Milim Nava's declaration of war on Eurazania.

The following simulation dates are now locked:

- T+151: CURRENT SYNC POINT. Carrion still publicly rules an intact Eurazania capital. Milim has NOT yet publicly declared war on Eurazania. Clayman is alive. Rimuru is not yet publicly known as an awakened True Demon Lord. The future destruction of the Eurazania capital is not player knowledge.
- T+153: crisis pressure around Tempest/Falmuth enters the active-event window. Exact hidden military/political actions remain world-side until causally observable; this date is not automatic player knowledge.
- T+154: Milim publicly appears over Eurazania and declares war on Carrion. Carrion begins/accelerates evacuation of the capital. This is a huge local public event and therefore becomes rapidly observable in the Eurazania capital; no artificial information blackout is permitted.
- T+154 through T+160: evacuation week / countdown to the announced clash. Civil, military, trade, road, lodging, festival and refugee systems react independently. Player projects are affected naturally.
- T+160: Arlequino's previously scheduled 'Фестиваль Эуразании: Неделя силы' is still scheduled to begin. It is NOT protected from the canonical crisis. By this date the capital is in evacuation/emergency conditions; whether the festival is cancelled, transformed, relocated, delayed or partially attempted must emerge causally from decisions by Carrion, organizers, participants and Arlequino. Do not auto-cancel it merely because the GM knows future canon.
- T+161: CANON ANCHOR — 'Day of Destruction' / Carrion versus Milim; Milim's Drago Nova destroys the Eurazania capital. Carrion is publicly presumed defeated/dead afterward, while his true survival remains secret until canonically revealed. This event occurs unless prior player/world intervention has produced a genuinely causally sufficient divergence.

The T+154 -> T+161 seven-day interval is selected to match the canonical description that Carrion ordered evacuation during the week before the battle. Canon sources also describe Milim as declaring war in advance and giving Eurazania time to prepare.

## Tempest/Falmuth parallel track
The Falmuth/Western Holy Church attack on Tempest and Rimuru's return/counterattack unfold in the same crisis window around the Eurazania evacuation and destruction. Exact T+ hours for every Tempest-side sub-event are intentionally not over-specified in v1 because canon does not give a complete universal clock. Their ORDER must remain canonical:

1. hostile anti-monster/Falmuth-Church operation hits Tempest while Rimuru is absent;
2. Rimuru returns and discovers the casualties;
3. Rimuru destroys the Falmuth army and initiates his awakening as a True Demon Lord;
4. after awakening/revival events, Veldora is released;
5. news of Eurazania's destruction and Carrion's apparent defeat reaches Rimuru;
6. Clayman's further moves lead toward the Eurazania campaign and Walpurgis;
7. Walpurgis ends with Clayman's death, Carrion/Frey later stepping down, and the Eight Star Demon Lords / Octagram configuration.

Future canon events receive exact T+ dates only when the active story approaches them and the preceding intervals can be mapped without contradiction.

## Divergence rule
Canon is the autonomous baseline, not an invulnerable railroad.

- Canonical actors keep their canonical goals, knowledge, capabilities and planned actions unless the living world gives them a causal reason to change.
- Arlequino may influence events if he actually learns about them and acts in time.
- The GM may not protect canon by making NPCs irrational or immune to consequences.
- The GM may not protect Arlequino by postponing Milim, Falmuth, Clayman or other canonical actors until his personal plans finish.
- A true divergence must be earned by observable actions and consequences, then persisted as a branch of this world's history.

## Knowledge boundary
This timeline file is GM/runtime knowledge. Arlequino does NOT automatically know future dates/events. He learns current/past events only through perception, public announcements, messengers, travelers, guilds, officials, letters, rumors, witnesses or other causal channels.

This rule works together with `runtime/rules/NEWS_RUMOR_PROPAGATION_v1.md` and does not override player-agency rules.
