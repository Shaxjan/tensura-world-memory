# T+160 — Arlequino on love in his lineage

During the Dwargon hall conversation, Arlequino stated that for a member of his lineage/house to genuinely fall in love is extremely rare — something that happens roughly once in a hundred years.

Context:
- This was said directly after Arlequino remarked that his older brother had advised him to marry a woman who loved him rather than the woman he loved.
- Arlequino framed his mutual love with Rena as an exceptional event for his family line.

Knowledge boundary:
- The statement was made openly in the hall and was heard by Rena and the currently gathered 22 students.
