# T+160 — Arlequino theatrically tells «Зов Ктулху»

In the Dwargon theater hall, after repeated joking references to Cthulhu/R'lyeh, Arlequino finally began a full theatrical, dark retelling of «Зов Ктулху» to the gathered group.

Audience/knowledge boundary:
- The currently gathered 22 students hear the telling.
- Rena hears it while sitting on Arlequino's lap under his dark-green high-collared cloak.
- Fira, Tia, and Mayra are present as part of the 22.

Narrative beats conveyed in the telling include the recognizable structure of «Зов Ктулху»: fragmentary investigation through inherited notes and testimony; disturbing dreams and an alien city; a secret cult worshipping Cthulhu; the name R'lyeh; a maritime expedition reaching a risen cyclopean city; Cthulhu emerging; survivors fleeing and the city sinking again; and the final implication that the horror is dormant rather than truly gone.

Arlequino performs it theatrically and ominously rather than reading flatly.
