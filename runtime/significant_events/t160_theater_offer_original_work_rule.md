# T+160 — Theater offer: original-work standard

Scene context: Dwargon hall, Arlequino speaking to the 19 students currently present.

Arlequino offered them the possibility of becoming part of his theater.

When asked whether they would be allowed to create their own work there, Arlequino answered:

> там это везде. Свое если это гениальное то да.

Canonical meaning:
- Original work is allowed in Arlequino's theater.
- Being original is not enough by itself; Arlequino expects the work to be genuinely strong / exceptional (his wording: "если это гениальное").
- This is part of the offer currently heard by the 19 students in the hall.
- Rena, Fira, Tia, and Mayra are still absent retrieving the manuscript "Гвазимодо" and did not hear this directly.

Do not infer acceptance by any student unless they explicitly accept later.
