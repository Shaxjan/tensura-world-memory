# T+153 — Rena given authority to choose assistant

At T+153 ~08:11, in the kitchen at 18 Old Garden Street, Arlequino explicitly told Rena to choose the female assistant herself: «помощницу выбирай сама».

Interpretation:
- Rena now has delegated authority to select and hire the assistant for her work without requiring Arlequino to make the final choice.
- This does not establish a specific candidate, salary, contract, start date, or hire. Those remain unknown until causally established.
- The prior preference remains: Rena wants someone competent enough to take routine/admin work without constant supervision.
