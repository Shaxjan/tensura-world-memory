# T+160 — Arlequino explains origin/purpose of three Eurazania support songs

Time: approximately T+160 ~19:43–19:44.
Location: Dwargon hall, Arlequino on stage with 19 students remaining in the hall.

Arlequino explicitly states that these three songs were composed and sung by him on the move immediately after Demon Lord Milim declared war on the people of Eurazania:
1. «Страна Огромная»
2. the Carrion war-ballad / «Гений военного дела»
3. «Звериная Армия» / «Звериная Армия всех сильней»

He explicitly defines these songs as his support for Eurazania.

Knowledge boundary:
- The 19 students currently remaining in the hall hear this statement directly.
- Rena, Fira, Tia, and Mayra are away retrieving the manuscript «Гвазимодо» and do NOT hear this statement directly unless later told.
- This statement does not imply any additional hidden wartime facts beyond what Arlequino explicitly says.
