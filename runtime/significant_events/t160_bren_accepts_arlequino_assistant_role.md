# T+160 — Bren accepts Arlequino's offer to leave Dwargon as his assistant

## Scene
- Location: Dwargon hall, stage gathering with Arlequino's students.
- Present in hall: Arlequino and the students who remained after Rena, Fira, Tia and Mayra left to fetch the manuscript «Гвазимодо».

## Offer and acceptance
Arlequino, while struggling with Bren's name and calling him «Берн», offered Bren to later leave Dwargon with him, become his assistant, and participate in Arlequino's concerts.

Arlequino clarified the assistant role as:
- handling small errands and practical tasks;
- helping when Arlequino cannot be everywhere at once;
- covering routine matters when Arlequino becomes absorbed in creative work for several days;
- concert participation remains part of the opportunity.

Bren accepted and said he wants to try.
