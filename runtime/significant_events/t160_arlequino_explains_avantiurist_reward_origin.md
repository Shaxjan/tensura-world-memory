# T+160 — Arlequino explains the origin of «Авантюрист» to the students

Scene: Dwargon hall, ~T+160 19:48, after Arlequino performed «Авантюрист» while the students were finishing written introductions.

Arlequino explicitly says:

> эту песню я сделал наградой для авантюристов блюмунда 😊. Я поставил задание распространить листовки с моими концертами и песнями и если число дойдет до отмеченного я напишу песню про авантюристов. 1 листовка 1 медяк вроде.

Canonical meaning:
- «Авантюрист» was created as a reward for the adventurers of Blumund.

- He had promised that if the leaflet count reached a specified target, he would write a song about adventurers.
- Arlequino currently remembers the leaflet payment only uncertainly as approximately/possibly 1 copper per leaflet ("1 листовка 1 медяк вроде"). Do NOT harden this into a verified exact rate unless older authoritative records or a later user correction confirm it.
- The exact target leaflet count is not stated here and remains UNKNOWN.
