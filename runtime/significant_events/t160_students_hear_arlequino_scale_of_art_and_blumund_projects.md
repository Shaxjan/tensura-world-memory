# T+160 — Students hear Arlequino's scale-of-art speech and Blumund projects

At approximately T+160 ~19:28 in a Dwargon hall, Arlequino addressed the 22 students currently assembled there, with Rena also present.

Arlequino told them that they were thinking too small: small performances, small earnings, and being recognized by only a few dozen people were not enough. He urged them to aim to be known on the level of a king or greater, saying their only true limits were their own souls and that creators are as great as their imagination.

He explicitly framed performances drawing around one thousand to several thousand people as small-scale by his current standards, and major concerts as something larger.

He also directly stated in front of the assembled students and Rena that he had:
- written 5 books;
- wanted to begin selling them in a run of 100,000 copies;
- organized regular poetry evenings;
- in Blumund, organized weekly and daily mail/publication carrying poems by poets and poetesses.

Knowledge boundary: these statements were heard by the 22 students currently present in the hall and by Rena. Do not infer that the four absent students heard this unless later told in-world.

This is a direct in-world statement from Arlequino and should be treated as character knowledge for those witnesses.
