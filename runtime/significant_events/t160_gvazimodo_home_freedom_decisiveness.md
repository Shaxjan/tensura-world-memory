# T+160 — authorial clarification on Gvazimodo and home

In the Dwargon hall discussion of «Гвазимодо», a student asked what is needed for someone to stop calling a place home when that place has harmed and shaped them badly.

Arlequino answered while continuing to hold/kiss Rena:

> «свобода и решительность! Да так и есть»

Canonical interpretation: for this discussion, Arlequino explicitly affirms that freedom and decisiveness are what allow someone like Gvazimodo to break from a harmful conception of home.
