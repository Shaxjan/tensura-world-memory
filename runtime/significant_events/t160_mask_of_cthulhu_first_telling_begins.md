## First story now canonically told: «Возвращение Хастура»

Established beats heard by Rena and all 22 students:
- Amos Tuttle lives in an old estate in a western human principality, away from major roads.
- Before death he orders Haddon to destroy the house and books, leave the lower chambers unopened, and return one especially dangerous book without reading it.
- Paul Tuttle inherits and refuses to destroy the estate immediately.
- Hidden stairs lead beneath the house into wet subterranean passages.
- Paul's reading reveals the Great Old Ones, including Cthulhu and Hastur, and Amos's promise to provide Hastur with an «убежище».
- Amos's corpse changes and vanishes from its sealed crypt.
- Something using Amos's voice climbs beneath the house; later Paul learns that «убежище» means a living host/body, not a physical building.
- Paul orders Haddon to prepare to destroy the estate and to ignore any later voice asking him to stop.
- In the cavern, Paul encounters the altered body of Amos and a vast presence in the black water.
- The entity mimics Paul's voice to countermand his order; Haddon follows the previously agreed instruction instead.
- The estate and underground passages are destroyed/collapsed; water floods the ruins; Hastur and a presence tied to Cthulhu appear to collide or oppose one another.
- Neither Amos nor Paul's body is recovered.
- Haddon later finds an unexplained note: «Дом разрушен. Дверь — нет».
