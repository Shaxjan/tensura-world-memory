# T+153 — Student housing selection for 22 Dwargon students

At approximately T+153 10:09–10:10 in Eurazania capital, Arlequino and Rena completed inspection of two housing options for the expected 22 Dwargon students attending the festival.

## Option 1 — former small boarding house
- 12 rooms.
- Large common hall suitable for meals, meetings, and rehearsals/classes.
- Larger kitchen.
- 2 washrooms.
- Small courtyard.
- Suitable to house all 22 under one roof without excessive crowding.
- Price: 0g 90s for 10 days.
- Refundable deposit: 0g 20s.
- Extra day: 0g 09s.
- Owner said the quoted terms remain available until noon on T+153.

## Option 2
- 9 rooms in main building + 3 small rooms in an outbuilding.
- Smaller kitchen.
- No proper large common hall.
- 1 full washroom inside plus a wash area outside.
- Closer to the southern festival grounds.
- Price: 0g 75s for 10 days.
- Refundable deposit: 0g 15s.
- Extra day: 0g 08s.

## Decision
Arlequino explicitly chose Option 1. Reason: he does not want students to have excuses that poor rooms prevented rehearsal. He also recalled that in Dwargon some guitar students had once crowded more than 10–12 people into one room and rehearsed all night taking turns on a single guitar.

Rena had independently preferred Option 1 as the more practical house for 22 students.

Important: selection is decided, but the deposit/payment/contract has not yet been physically completed in-scene at the moment of this record. Do not deduct money until payment actually occurs.
