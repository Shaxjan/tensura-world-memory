# T+160 — Arlequino quotes his older brother's marriage advice

In the Dwargon theater hall, Arlequino told Rena and the gathered 22 students that his older brother once advised him:

> «Мелкий женись на той которая любит тебя на не на той которую любишь...»

Arlequino then added that it "worked out badly" in the sense that he loves the woman who loves him — clearly referring to Rena.

Durable character/family continuity:
- Arlequino has an older brother.
- The older brother called him «Мелкий» in this remembered advice.
- The advice was to marry the woman who loves him rather than the woman he loves.
- Arlequino now frames his marriage to Rena as both mutual love and a playful contradiction of that advice.

Knowledge boundary: Rena and all 22 currently gathered students heard this statement directly.
