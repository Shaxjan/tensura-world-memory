# T+160 — Nineteen students publicly introduced by Arlequino

Location: Dwargon hall, stage. Approx. T+160 ~19:57–20:05.

Arlequino had received 19 written self-introductions from students currently present in the hall and then read all 19 aloud, adding spontaneous comments after many entries. This makes the following names, instruments, self-descriptions, and public knowledge durable canon for the 19 present students.

1. Yora — guitar. Last two years: taverns and small halls; learned to hold attention in noisy rooms; admits mostly repeating what already worked instead of attempting something larger.
2. Seli — violin. Worked on technique, small arrangements, helped younger students. Wrote: «Хочу сочинить вещь, которую люди будут узнавать с первой фразы».
3. Orn — flute. Paid gigs: festivals, dances, noisy venues; good improviser; tired of being «флейтистом, которого можно поставить куда угодно».
4. Bren — guitar. Performed and arranged; says he chose safe, small tasks too long; explicitly mentioned Arlequino’s same-day offer to try becoming his assistant. Arlequino misnamed him publicly again as «Берн».
5. Kera — violin. Played in small ensembles, copied parts; technically reliable; admits she created nothing of her own that genuinely frightened or excited her.
6. Dain — guitar. Taverns, caravan yards, workers’ festivals; knows requested songs well; familiar songs paid reliably, so he stopped taking risks.
7. Lessa — flute. Breath work and long tones; occasional ceremonies; wants flute to become the center of a piece rather than decoration.
8. Varr — guitar. Tried writing songs; abandoned most after first draft if they seemed ordinary; wrote: «Хочу хотя бы одну довести до конца, даже если сначала она будет плохой».
9. Nemi — violin. Plays dances and private celebrations; more interested in making people move than formal concerts; wants to build an entire evening through music.
10. Teren — flute. Mostly played in ensembles led by others; identifies waiting for someone else to decide what to do as his main problem.
11. Rika — guitar. Teaches beginners for money; explains fundamentals well and likes teaching; wants her own coherent teaching system rather than disconnected lessons.
12. Holn — violin. Long focused on speed and difficult passages; recently realized audiences often remember a simple melody better than technical complexity.
13. Vessa — flute. Collects melodies of workers, travelers, and Dwargon families; has many fragments recorded but has not yet developed most of them into finished work.
14. Kelm — guitar. Confident on stage, avoids his own lyrics because they look stupid to him on paper.
15. Arin — violin. Frequently accompanied singers; learned to feel breathing, pauses, and emotional changes in vocalists; wants to try vocal arrangements herself.
16. Dorel — flute. Experiments with sound in tunnels, stairwells, and stone spaces of Dwargon; interested in how space changes the same melody; has not yet turned this into a full performance.
17. Mina — guitar. Likes fast rhythm, clapping, communal singing; says she can entertain a crowd but wants to learn to lead it.
18. Orenk — violin. Repairs bows and helps others care for instruments; wrote: «Я стал полезен всем вокруг, но до сих пор не понимаю, каким артистом хочу быть сам».
19. Jessa — flute. Wrote several short melodies but shows them only to close acquaintances; wrote: «Я больше боюсь, что надо мной будут смеяться, чем того, что буду плоха. И понимаю, что это проблема».
