# T+160 — origin of «Гвазимодо» and Rena's influence

During the Dwargon hall discussion, Arlequino explained why he wrote «Гвазимодо».

Durable canon:
- He was angered by the Western Church's policy/doctrine that non-humans are monsters and enemies of humankind.
- He decided to write a work showing that humans themselves can be no different from the beings they call "monsters".
- His original conception was meant to contain much less romance.
- He then said that after Rena entered his life, the work changed: among the human "monsters" there appeared one pure, uncorrupted soul.
- He said this while holding/kissing Rena, making clear that he meant Rena.

Knowledge boundary: this explanation was heard directly by the 22 students currently gathered in the hall and by Rena, Fira, Tia, and Mayra.
