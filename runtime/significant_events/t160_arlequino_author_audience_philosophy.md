# T+160 — Arlequino on authorial intent and audience interpretation

In the Dwargon hall during discussion of «Гвазимодо», Arlequino rejected the question of what the audience "should" feel about Gvazimodo.

His durable position:
- An author does not have the right to decide what the audience must feel.
- The author can present a viewpoint and a narrative, but interpretation remains with the audience.
- Every story has untold parts and unanswered questions.
- Arlequino used examples from «Гвазимодо»: why Gvazimodo was abandoned as a child, who his parents were, and why Frollo desired Esmeralda specifically at that moment.
- He explicitly stated that with enough speculation one can make almost any character look guilty or innocent.

This is a teaching/philosophy statement heard directly by the 22 students currently gathered in the hall and by Rena, Fira, Tia, and Mayra.
