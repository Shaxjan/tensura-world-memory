# T+160 — 19 student introductions handed to Arlequino in Dwargon hall

Scene state: Rena, Fira, Tia and Mayra are away retrieving Arlequino's manuscript «Гвазимодо». Nineteen students remain in the hall and have each written a short self-introduction on paper at Arlequino's request. Arlequino reads them silently. The content below is now their durable self-described identity unless later corrected by the user.

1. Yora — guitar. Writes quickly and directly. Says she kept playing in taverns and small halls, learned to hold an audience without stopping when people talk over her, but admits she has mostly repeated what already worked instead of creating something bigger.
2. Seli — violin. Precise and orderly. Says she has spent the last two years improving technique, arranging short pieces for small ensembles and helping younger students with intonation. Adds by her own choice that she wants to compose something that people recognize from the first phrase.
3. Orn — flute. Writes his name large. Says he plays wherever he can get paid, has learned to improvise around dancers and noisy rooms, and wants to stop being merely "the flutist who fits in anywhere" and make something people come specifically to hear.
4. Bren — guitar. Says he has been performing and arranging, but has stayed too cautious and too small. Current new commitment: accepted in principle to try becoming Arlequino's assistant, handling small errands/practical support when Arlequino is overloaded or absorbed in creative work, and possibly participating in concerts. Compensation and logistics are not yet agreed.
5. Kera — violin. Says she has been working in small chamber groups and copying parts for other musicians. She is proud that she almost never makes technical mistakes, but admits she has not made anything of her own that frightened or excited her.
6. Dain — guitar. Says he plays in drinking houses, caravan yards and workers' gatherings. He knows many songs people ask for, earns steadily, and admits he stopped trying to surprise anyone because familiar songs pay reliably.
7. Lessa — flute. Says she has focused on breath control and long sustained phrases and sometimes plays for ceremonies. She wants to learn how to make a flute part feel like the center of a piece rather than decoration.
8. Varr — guitar. Says he tried writing songs, threw most of them away, and has a habit of abandoning work when the first version sounds ordinary. He writes that he wants to finish one song even if the first draft is bad.
9. Nemi — violin. Says she plays dances and private celebrations. She likes making people move more than playing formal recitals and wants to understand how to build a whole evening around music rather than just provide background.
10. Teren — flute. Says he has mostly played in ensembles and followed stronger leaders. He writes that his main weakness is waiting for someone else to decide what the group should do.
11. Rika — guitar. Says she has taught beginners for money, can explain basic technique well, and enjoys seeing people improve. She wants to create a proper teaching method instead of giving disconnected lessons.
12. Holn — violin. Says he has been obsessed with speed and difficult passages and only recently realized that listeners often remember a simple melody more than a difficult performance. He wants to learn restraint.
13. Vessa — flute. Says she has been collecting folk melodies from workers, travelers and families in Dwargon and writing them down. She has many fragments but has done almost nothing with the collection yet.
14. Kelm — guitar. Says he performs confidently but avoids writing lyrics because he thinks his words sound stupid on paper. He wants to stop hiding behind other people's songs.
15. Arin — violin. Says she has been playing accompaniment for singers and has become good at following breathing, phrasing and emotional shifts. She wants to try arranging for voices herself.
16. Dorel — flute. Says he likes experimenting with echoes, stairwells, tunnels and stone rooms because the same melody changes depending on space. He has never turned those experiments into a formal performance.
17. Mina — guitar. Says she likes fast, rhythmic playing and getting groups to clap or sing along. She wants to learn how to lead a crowd instead of merely entertaining whoever is already in front of her.
18. Orenk — violin. Says he repairs bows and helps other students maintain instruments in addition to playing. He writes that he has become useful to everyone but is unsure what kind of artist he himself wants to be.
19. Jessa — flute. Says she has written several short melodies but shows them only to close friends. Her final line: "I am more afraid of being laughed at than of being bad, and I know that is a problem."
