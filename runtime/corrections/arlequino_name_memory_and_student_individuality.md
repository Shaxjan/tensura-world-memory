# Canon correction — Arlequino's memory for names and student individuality

User-established canon:
- Maestro Arlequino is generally **bad at remembering people's names**. This is a stable personal trait, not limited to the Dwargon students.
- Forgetting a name does not mean the person was unnamed or unimportant; Arlequino may recognize the person, face, role, instrument, behavior or shared history while failing to recall the name.
- If Arlequino asks a person their name again, that is normal in-character behavior and the newly re-established name should then be persisted.
- The recovered Dwargon leaders remain **Fira (Фира), Mayra (Майра), Tia (Тиа)**.
- Names of other Dwargon students are currently not recovered from durable memory; treat this as Arlequino failing to remember them until he asks again, not as proof they never had names.
- Every student/NPC is an individual person with their own personality, reactions, preferences and voice. Do not flatten the students into one collective personality or make them interchangeable copies.
- Do not invent unrecovered names before an in-world reintroduction or user correction.
