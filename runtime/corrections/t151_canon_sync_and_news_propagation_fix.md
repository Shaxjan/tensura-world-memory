# T+151 correction — canon clock + living information network

Status: ACTIVE CORRECTION. User explicitly requested: fix the silent-news defect and synchronize the living world with Tensura canon by T+ dates.

Activation scene: T+151 ~16:52, Arlequino and Rena at home, 18 Old Garden Street, Eurazania capital. Activation itself advances no world time and does not alter their current physical scene.

## What was wrong
Causal NPC/player knowledge had been enforced without a sufficiently active transmission layer. The result made the wider world feel frozen/silent around Arlequino even though the project vision requires an autonomous world.

## Effective repair
Two rules are now authoritative for future play:

1. `runtime/rules/CANON_TIMELINE_SYNC_v1.md`
   - locks T+151 to the pre-Milim-declaration phase;
   - T+154 Milim declares war on Eurazania;
   - T+154..T+160 evacuation/countdown;
   - T+160 scheduled start of Arlequino's festival remains in-world and is allowed to collide with the crisis;
   - T+161 canonical Day of Destruction / destruction of the Eurazania capital unless a causally sufficient world divergence occurs;
   - canonical actors continue independently and never wait for player plans.

2. `runtime/rules/NEWS_RUMOR_PROPAGATION_v1.md`
   - adds autonomous rumor/news transmission through officials, guilds, merchants, caravans, travelers, refugees, streets, letters, messengers and established magical channels;
   - preserves causal knowledge and uncertainty;
   - gives major local events rapid public visibility;
   - gives Arlequino passive exposure to plausible news during ordinary life rather than requiring explicit interrogation every time;
   - repairs previous missing-news backlog prospectively without retconning secret knowledge into Arlequino's past.

## Important non-retcon
Arlequino does not suddenly remember facts he was never told. At activation, his established knowledge remains unchanged. Starting now, already-circulating public information can naturally reach him, and new canonical/world events propagate from their actual occurrence time.

## Festival collision
The previously saved festival date T+160 is NOT moved to protect it from canon. The world will react. Organizers, authorities, citizens and visitors may alter behavior as the crisis develops. Arlequino remains free to respond or ignore it.

## GM rule
Do not use OOC knowledge of the T+161 destruction as if Arlequino knows it. Do not suppress visible T+154 onward emergency conditions merely to preserve surprise. Distinguish player knowledge from world truth at all times.

This correction overrides prior de facto behavior in which the wider world produced little/no ambient information unless the player explicitly asked for it.
