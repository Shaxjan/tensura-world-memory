# T+154 — RETCON: amplified public speech immediately after «Священная война»

AUTHORITATIVE RETCON of the immediate aftermath of Arlequino's public anti-Milim war-song performance.

Time: ~T+154 07:33, Eurazania capital, crowded evacuation street.

Immediately after the song, Arlequino deliberately speaks very loudly and magically amplifies his voice so that it carries across a much wider section of the evacuation flow.

Exact player speech:

«а я разве не прав ? Прилетела , объявила войну, и им еще ее бояться ? Бояться чего? Что она их убьет? Так она уже объявила им войну ! Я верю в зверолюдей и в их воинственный нрав и в Кариона - это не народ трусов которые испугаются сильного врага ! 😡»

Interpretation/world logic:
- This is not private banter with Rena. It is a public wartime address deliberately broadcast by magic.
- Arlequino argues that fear of Milim cannot be allowed to define Eurazania after she has already declared war.
- He explicitly affirms faith in the beastmen's martial character and in Carrion, and rejects framing the population as cowards simply because the enemy is overwhelmingly strong.
- Reactions must fit Eurazania's warrior culture: fear and concern for families can coexist with pride, defiance, anger, loyalty to Carrion, and willingness to resist. Do not portray the population as generically timid.
- Magical amplification increases the number of direct witnesses substantially compared with the song alone, but does not make the speech instantly known to the whole city or to Milim. Subsequent spread remains causal through witnesses, soldiers, evacuation columns and rumor.
- Guards still prioritize evacuation order and may use the morale surge to keep civilians moving rather than allowing a static rally.
