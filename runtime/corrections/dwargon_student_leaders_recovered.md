# Recovered canon — Dwargon student leaders

Source recovery: initial World Memory save uploaded in commit `de7c0f9ad0625a2ebe90154526e574a73e8f84ad`.

Authoritative recovered facts:
- Arlequino taught 26 students in Dwargon.
- The three named student leaders are **Fira (Фира), Mayra (Майра), Tia (Тиа)**.
- The school had three ensembles/directions: **guitar, violin, flute**.
- Arlequino's formulas were: guitar = love, violin = soul, flute = freedom.
- Later recollection confirms the students split into the three instrument groups, each group had a soloist selected through audience choice, and the students later staged `Газель и Эмилия` and `ТоД`.

Important uncertainty:
- The surviving durable records do **not** establish which of Fira / Mayra / Tia corresponds to guitar, violin, or flute.
- The surviving durable records do **not** provide names for the other 23 students.
- Do not invent those mappings or names unless they are recovered from an older source or re-established by the user.

Continuity rule:
- Fira, Mayra, and Tia are active historical canon NPCs from Arlequino's Dwargon school and should not be replaced by newly invented leaders.
