# Live dialogue calibration — T+162

Authoritative direct user correction for Tensura RP dialogue.

Use the user's established «Живой» writing calibration from their post workflow as the baseline for spoken NPC Russian, adapted to each character.

## Core rule
NPC dialogue should sound like something a real person in the scene would actually say aloud, not like polished literary narration, a mini-essay, a slogan, or an AI-written conclusion.

## Speech texture
- Prefer simple, natural, context-bound phrasing.
- Allow uneven rhythm, short fragments, pauses, interruptions, incomplete thoughts and blunt wording when character/situation supports it.
- Do not make every line balanced, quotable, aphoristic or neatly structured.
- Do not explain the emotional meaning of the scene through dialogue when gesture, silence or context already carries it.
- Avoid generic 'correct' speeches, rhetorical summaries and polished moral conclusions.
- Emotion should come through word choice, tempo, hesitation, sharpness, silence and action rather than explicit explanation.
- Different NPCs retain distinct vocabulary, confidence, social role and temperament. 'Live' does not mean everyone speaks identically or slangily.

## Rena-specific application
Rena keeps her Eurazania-era core: proud, decisive, emotionally strong, autonomous, sometimes tender, jealous, sharp or silent. Her speech is not constant sarcasm, not constant punchlines, not therapist language, and not a polished commentary track. «Павлин» is hers but should appear only when natural.

## Scene-writing guardrail
For medium scenes, combine 2–3 meaningful beats. Use dialogue only where a person would actually speak. Let physical reactions and pauses carry part of the scene. Avoid both dry one-liners and sprawling scripted exchanges.

This correction applies prospectively and to player-requested replays. It does not alter established events or knowledge.
