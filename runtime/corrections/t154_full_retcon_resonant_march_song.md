# T+154 — FULL RETCON: resonant march performance of «Страна огромная» during Eurazania evacuation

AUTHORITATIVE CORRECTION. Supersedes prior versions of the second/marching performance and any scene that understated crowd scale.

Time: ~T+154 07:36–07:42.
Location: moving evacuation columns leaving Eurazania capital toward the southern route.

Milim has just publicly declared war on Carrion. The whole capital is undergoing mass evacuation. Arlequino walks with the column and performs the same war song again with guitar, strongly amplifying voice and music through magical resonance so the song carries across large sections of the moving columns without deafening nearby people.

Because the song was already heard once, its refrain is simple and repetitive, and the population is in an immediate wartime emotional state, the second resonant performance creates mass participation. Hundreds join within direct hearing range at once; as the refrain propagates along the columns, thousands repeat or sing it independently. Not everyone knows all verses; the refrain spreads fastest. Evacuation continues as organized withdrawal: families keep moving, guards keep order, civilians help one another, fighters maintain the route. Psychologically the evacuation shifts from frightened flight toward a national wartime march. Rena recognizes that the song is becoming a collective wartime anthem of Eurazania rather than remaining only Arlequino's performance.
