# T+153 — Public canon backlog integration

Status: ACTIVE CORRECTION / CONTINUITY RULE.

User clarified that when asking what canon has already happened and what news/rumors should exist, this includes earlier major canonical events, not only the current Falmuth/Tempest crisis window.

From T+153 onward, the simulation must naturally integrate the backlog of major pre-T+153 public or semi-public canonical events that should already exist in regional knowledge pools, especially in Eurazania and neighboring states.

Examples include, without limiting the rule to these:
- the Orc Lord / Orc Disaster crisis and large-scale orc war in Jura;
- the defeat of the Orc Disaster and the subsequent incorporation/reorganization of surviving orcs under Tempest-aligned order;
- the rise and growing regional visibility of Jura Tempest Federation;
- Milim Nava's prior contact and association with Rimuru/Tempest;
- the Charybdis incident, including its regional-scale notoriety and the fact that Eurazania figures such as Phobio were directly involved;
- other comparably large canonical incidents that predate the current T+153 crisis window and would plausibly have generated persistent public memory, rumors, guild knowledge, traveler stories, political awareness, or local folklore.

Knowledge boundary:
- Do NOT retcon Arlequino as having secretly known these events all along.
- Do NOT give NPCs omniscient access to hidden causal details such as Clayman's full manipulation chain unless they had a valid source.
- Public-scale facts, famous names, broad outcomes, visible consequences and long-circulating stories may surface naturally now, including with NPC surprise that Arlequino missed widely discussed events.
- Fine-grained secret motives and backstage responsibility remain secret/restricted unless leaked or learned causally.

Presentation:
- Integrate backlog through ordinary scenes: Borgh, Meira, guilds, merchants, travelers, performers, students, officials, taverns, road talk, festival logistics, etc.
- Avoid detached lore dumps unless Arlequino explicitly asks for a briefing/history.
- Distinguish established public fact, reliable secondary report, rumor, wild rumor, and hidden truth.

This correction supplements NEWS_RUMOR_PROPAGATION_v1.md and CANON_TIMELINE_SYNC_v1.md. It does not advance game time.
