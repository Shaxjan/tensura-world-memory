# Arlequino — cloak continuity

- Arlequino has a dark green cloak with a long collar.
- When he throws this cloak over Rena, the silhouette/visual reads as villain-like; in the T+160 Dwargon theater scene he called her «моя злодейка 😈» specifically because of how she looked under this cloak.
- This supersedes the mistaken interpretation that «злодейка» referred to the room-booking joke.
