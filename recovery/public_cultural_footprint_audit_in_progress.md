## Festival / tournament
Recovered festival name: **«Фестиваль Эуразании: Неделя силы»**.
At T+131, 29 days remained until the festival.

Earlier, at **T+130 ~18:43–18:44**, Arlequino had already publicly promoted the festival and tournament to a large crowd in a training yard.

Publicly announced tournament conditions:
- winner receives gold;
- winner earns the right to fight King Carrion;
- winner receives a separate Crown reward;
- monetary prizes also go to the final ten;
- tournament publicly declared open to those who wish to participate;
- Borga designated as the person responsible for participation questions.

Rena and Borga directly heard this announcement.

Simulation consequence: «Неделя силы» was already a public campaign, not merely a private idea.

## Book/publication campaign
At **T+153**, Arlequino instructed Meira to spread news widely about upcoming publication of the Cthulhu works — markets, fighting arenas, magic guilds, adventurer guilds, effectively everywhere.

Planned publication order:
1. «Зов Ктулху»
2. «Маска Ктулху»
3. «Газель и Эмилия»
4. «Лейла и безумец»

A proposed print run of **50,000 copies** for «Зов Ктулху» existed, but this is a **proposal**, not confirmed completed production.

This creates a separate plausible propagation path among refugees and must not be forgotten.
