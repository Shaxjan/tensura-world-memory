# 4. T+130 direct recollection of how the school began

LIVE v144 preserves Arlequino's own late-night recollection.

Confirmed recollection:
- he gathered people at **a Dwargon concert hall at 07:00** by announcing free lessons in guitar, violin, and flute;
- after they gathered, he forced a choice of one instrument direction and the people split into groups;
- he gave his three instruments to those groups and ordered each group to create music by the next morning;
- spectators attended the lessons;
- the next morning he initially framed spectator selection as determining who deserved to be called musicians, but ultimately kept everyone;
- next stage: the three groups created music together, each with a soloist who had won the audience choice, again by the next morning.

Primary source:
- LIVE v144, commit `cd6207a25981510868a8a44e709dbbeed2467ea1`.

Important boundary:
- the `concert hall at 07:00` in this recollection is the place where people were gathered for the school setup;
- surviving text does **not** identify that hall as `Каменная арка` or as the later second concert hall;
- do not merge those venue identities.

# 5. Student theatre layer: «Газель и Эмилия» and «Тод»

LIVE v144 adds a larger old-school performance layer that the compressed T+113 snapshot did not fully spell out.

Confirmed:

- «Газель и Эмилия» came **before** «Тод» in this recollection.

Source:
- LIVE v144, commit `cd6207a25981510868a8a44e709dbbeed2467ea1`.

This establishes that the old Dwargon school was not only musical; it also produced public/staged theatre work.
