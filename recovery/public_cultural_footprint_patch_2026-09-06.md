## DWARGON — confirmed pre-T+111 cultural layer

### School
- Arlequino taught **26 students**.
- Principle: **«Искусство принадлежит всем»**.
- First 3 group lessons were free; later group lessons cost 20c.
- Named leaders: **Fira, Mayra, Tia**.
- Second assignment formed three ensembles: **guitar / violin / flute**.

The old T+113 context explicitly confirms a concert titled:

**«Маэстро Арлекино и не только»**

It was connected with the second student assignment and the three ensembles.

### Instruments

Arlequino later left instruments to the students as gifts.

### «Газель и Эмилия»
- Public premiere in Dwargon is confirmed.
- Arlequino earned **3g**.
- The money was used to settle instrument costs.
- The instruments were left to students.
- The work left a continuing cultural trace.

Cross-city propagation is directly evidenced: at T+113 in Blumund, a man present near the palace had personally seen the Dwargon premiere. He remembered a divided audience reaction — part arguing that the tragedy could have been solved by one conversation, part crying; he described himself as belonging to both halves.

## SIMULATION CONSEQUENCE

Blumund songs with pre-concert exposure must not be treated as first heard at T+116. The Free Guild in particular already had collective familiarity with several adventurer songs.

«Газель и Эмилия» has a confirmed propagation path:

**Dwargon public premiere -> traveler/witness reaches Blumund -> separate Blumund public reading.**

This proves cross-city cultural transmission without implying universal fame.
